# Claim 4 limitations and deviations

The paper suppresses universal constants. Thus `alpha` and `c alpha` mean
order dependence, not equality: the exact worst-case biases are `2alpha` and
`(c+1)alpha`. Since `c>=1`, the latter lies between `c alpha` and
`2c alpha`.

The historical page's unexplained ratio near `c^2` is rejected. A `c^2` ratio
applies to squared upper-bound terms, not to the conditional-mean biases.
