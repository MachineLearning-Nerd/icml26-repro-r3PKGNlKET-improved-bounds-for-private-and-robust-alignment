# Claim 5 limitations and deviations

The verdict is **FALSIFIED** only because the broad first-online-private
subclaim has a dated, scope-matched counterexample. It does not assert that
PrivXPO's rate is false; that rate is independently verified. It also does not
claim the earlier POKL-RLHF algorithm has the same rate or coverability
assumptions.

No pre-target counterexample was found for the narrower “first online method
handling both local privacy and Huber corruption” statement. Literature
novelty is open-world, so this narrower point remains a limitation.

The finite implementation is a conformance fixture, not empirical proof of
the theorem. The universal rate evidence is the machine-checked theorem
instantiation.
